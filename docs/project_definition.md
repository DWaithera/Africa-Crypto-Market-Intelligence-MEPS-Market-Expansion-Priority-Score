\# MEPS — Market Expansion Priority Score



\## Business Question



Which African markets should a crypto exchange prioritize for expansion?



\## Objective



Build a data-driven scoring framework that ranks African markets according to their attractiveness for crypto exchange expansion.



\## Target Markets



\- Kenya

\- Nigeria

\- Ghana

\- South Africa



\## Scoring Dimensions



\### 1. Crypto Demand

Measures consumer interest and crypto activity.



\### 2. Financial Access

Measures the population's ability to access digital financial services.



\### 3. Market Potential

Measures the size and digital readiness of the potential customer base.



\### 4. Crypto Infrastructure

Measures the maturity and accessibility of the crypto ecosystem.



\## Final Output



A Market Expansion Priority Score (MEPS) for each country, producing a ranked list of markets for expansion.



\## Decision



The model should answer:



1\. Which market ranks first?

2\. Why does it rank first?

3\. Which factors drive its score?

4\. Which markets represent secondary opportunities?

5\. What weaknesses could prevent expansion?
