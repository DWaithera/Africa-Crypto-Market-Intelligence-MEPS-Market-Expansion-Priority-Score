\# MEPS Scoring Methodology



\## Objective



Convert multiple market indicators into a comparable score that ranks African markets by crypto exchange expansion potential.



\---



\## 1. Scoring Dimensions



MEPS consists of four dimensions:



| Dimension | Weight |

|---|---:|

| Crypto Demand | 30% |

| Financial Access | 25% |

| Market Potential | 25% |

| Crypto Infrastructure | 20% |

| \*\*Total\*\* | \*\*100%\*\* |



\---



\## 2. Metric Normalization



Because the indicators use different units, each metric will be normalized to a 0–100 scale.



For positive indicators:



score = 100 × (x - min(x)) / (max(x) - min(x))



For negative indicators:



score = 100 × (max(x) - x) / (max(x) - min(x))



Where:



\- x = country's observed value

\- min(x) = minimum value across target markets

\- max(x) = maximum value across target markets



\---



\## 3. Dimension Scores



\### Crypto Demand



Inputs:



\- Crypto search interest

\- Bitcoin search interest

\- USDT search interest



Dimension score:



\- Crypto search interest: 40%

\- Bitcoin search interest: 30%

\- USDT search interest: 30%



\---



\### Financial Access



Inputs:



\- Account ownership

\- Mobile money usage

\- Digital payments



Dimension score:



\- Account ownership: 35%

\- Mobile money usage: 35%

\- Digital payments: 30%



\---



\### Market Potential



Inputs:



\- Population

\- Internet penetration

\- GDP per capita



Dimension score:



\- Population: 40%

\- Internet penetration: 35%

\- GDP per capita: 25%



\---



\### Crypto Infrastructure



Inputs:



\- Crypto market activity

\- Regulatory environment

\- Exchange accessibility



Dimension score:



\- Crypto market activity: 40%

\- Regulatory environment: 30%

\- Exchange accessibility: 30%



\---



\## 4. Final MEPS Score



The final score is calculated as:



MEPS =



(Crypto Demand × 0.30)

\+

(Financial Access × 0.25)

\+

(Market Potential × 0.25)

\+

(Crypto Infrastructure × 0.20)



The maximum score is 100.



\---



\## 5. Ranking



Markets are ranked from highest to lowest MEPS score.



| Score | Interpretation |

|---:|---|

| 80–100 | Very High Priority |

| 60–79 | High Priority |

| 40–59 | Moderate Priority |

| 20–39 | Low Priority |

| 0–19 | Very Low Priority |



\---



\## 6. Important Limitations



MEPS is a decision-support framework, not a prediction of future crypto adoption.



The score is sensitive to:



\- Indicator selection

\- Weight selection

\- Data availability

\- Data quality

\- Country selection

\- Regulatory interpretation



Sensitivity analysis should therefore be performed to determine whether the market ranking changes substantially under alternative weighting assumptions.
