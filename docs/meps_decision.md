# MEPS Decision Definition

## 1. Purpose

The Market Expansion Priority Score (MEPS) is a data-driven market prioritization framework designed to support African expansion decisions for a crypto or fintech company.

MEPS answers:

> **If expansion resources are limited, which African market should the company prioritize first, and what evidence supports that decision?**

The framework converts multiple market indicators into a transparent comparative score and ranking, then connects the result to market intelligence and potential growth actions.

---

## 2. Business Decision

The primary decision is:

**Where should the company prioritize further expansion investigation and commercial resources?**

The output is a ranked set of markets accompanied by the evidence behind each ranking.

The ranking is intended to support decisions such as:

* Which market should receive deeper commercial research?
* Where should business development efforts be prioritized?
* Where should customer acquisition experiments be considered?
* Which markets warrant partnership, community, or localization efforts?
* Which markets require further investigation before investment?

---

## 3. Decision Unit

The unit of analysis is:

**Country-market**

Each country is evaluated independently using the same indicator framework.

Initial markets:

* Ghana
* Kenya
* Nigeria
* South Africa

The framework is designed to support expansion to additional African markets when comparable data becomes available.

---

## 4. What MEPS Measures

MEPS measures the **relative attractiveness and expansion priority of a market** based on selected indicators representing:

1. Market Attractiveness
2. Digital Readiness
3. Financial Accessibility
4. Crypto Demand
5. Crypto Activity
6. Growth Potential

These dimensions are hypotheses at this stage and will be challenged before the final framework is frozen.

---

## 5. What MEPS Does Not Measure

MEPS is not intended to:

* Predict future revenue
* Predict future crypto adoption
* Guarantee commercial success
* Determine the universally "best" African market
* Replace country-level due diligence
* Replace regulatory, legal, competitive, or operational assessment
* Establish causal relationships between indicators and business outcomes

A high MEPS score means that a market has a stronger **relative priority** under the selected methodology and assumptions.

---

## 6. Decision Horizon

The framework uses recent historical and current market indicators to assess relative market opportunity.

Where historical data is available, changes over time will be considered to identify market growth or improvement.

The exact analytical period may differ by indicator depending on source availability and will be documented explicitly.

---

## 7. Decision Output

The final MEPS output will provide:

### Market ranking

A ranked comparison of the markets included in the analysis.

### Dimension-level explanation

The contribution of each major dimension to the overall market score.

### Market intelligence

Evidence explaining why a market ranks highly or poorly.

### Growth activation

Potential implications for:

* Target segments
* User problems
* Relevant use cases
* Acquisition channels
* Partnerships
* KOLs
* Communities
* Events
* Activation and retention

### Business recommendation

A concise recommendation describing:

**where to prioritize, why, and what should happen next.**

---

## 8. Decision Principle

MEPS follows a simple principle:

> **Prioritize markets where the evidence suggests stronger expansion opportunity, accessibility, existing market signals, and future growth potential—while explicitly accounting for data limitations and methodological uncertainty.**

MEPS is therefore a **decision-support framework**, not an automated decision-maker.

---

## 9. Analytical Principle

Every indicator included in MEPS must have:

* A clear business question
* A defensible reason for inclusion
* A measurable definition
* A credible data source
* Comparable measurements across markets
* A defined direction of impact
* A documented limitation

Indicators will also be reviewed for correlation and conceptual overlap to reduce double counting.
